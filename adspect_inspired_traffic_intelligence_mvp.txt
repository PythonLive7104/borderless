==================================================
1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW
==================================================

Build a modern SaaS platform for traffic intelligence, fraud detection, visitor analysis, campaign monitoring, conversion tracking, and traffic-quality rules.

The product is inspired by the general category and capabilities of Adspect, but must use original branding, UI, code, copy, and architecture.

Core positioning:

"See every visitor. Score every click. Protect every campaign."

Primary users:
- Performance marketers
- Media buyers
- Affiliate marketers
- Agencies
- E-commerce businesses
- Advertisers
- Developers

The MVP must allow a customer to:
1. Create an account.
2. Create an organization/workspace.
3. Add a website.
4. Install a JavaScript tracking script.
5. Create campaigns.
6. Receive visitor/click data.
7. Analyze visitor and traffic signals.
8. Generate a risk score.
9. Classify traffic.
10. Create traffic-quality rules.
11. View traffic analytics.
12. Track conversions.
13. Manage API keys and webhooks.
14. Manage subscription and usage.
15. Manage account/security settings.

Important product boundary:
The MVP should focus on legitimate traffic-quality, fraud detection, analytics, security, and campaign protection. Do not implement features designed to deceive ad-network reviewers, bypass advertising-platform enforcement, or evade platform policies.

- Normalize visitor information

31. RISK ENGINE
==================================================

MVP should start with deterministic rules rather than machine learning.

Example weighted signals:

Known bot:
+50

Datacenter:
+25

Proxy:
+15

Suspicious automation:
+25

Abnormal request rate:
+20

Repeated suspicious activity:
+15

Normalize final score to 0-100.

Classification:

0-39:
human/low risk

40-69:
medium risk

70-84:
suspicious/high risk

85-100:
critical risk

The exact weights must be configurable and validated against real data.

Do not claim a specific detection accuracy until it has been measured using a representative labeled dataset.

==================================================
44. MVP EXCLUSIONS
==================================================

Do NOT include in the first MVP:

- Complex machine learning
- Huge proprietary IP database
- Dozens of third-party integrations
- Advanced reverse proxy infrastructure
- Website mirroring
- Ad-reviewer deception
- Advertising-platform enforcement evasion
- Fake CAPTCHA systems
- Automatic cloaking designed to hide prohibited content
- Enterprise white labeling
- Advanced predictive analytics
- Multi-region distributed traffic infrastructure

These can be considered only after the core product is stable, tested, and compliant.
